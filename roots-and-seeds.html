06 — What you're actually paying for

The platforms are ready.
That's the point.

BreatheHR and Xero being live doesn't make this cheaper — it means what we build is production-ready infrastructure from day one, not a prototype that needs replacing in 12 months.

Architecture expertise

BreatheHR's API, Make.com multi-site logic, WhatsApp Cloud API compliance and Claude prompt engineering are four distinct technical disciplines. Getting them to work reliably together — not just in a demo, but at 7am on a Monday with 40 staff across 4 sites — is the work.

Hallucination-proofing

An AI that tells a staff member they have 12 days holiday when they have 4 is worse than no AI at all. Significant build time goes into constraining the assistant to only answer from live BreatheHR data — never from assumption, never from memory. That's not default behaviour. That's engineered.

Built to scale

Every automation is architected for Site 5, Site 6, and beyond. Adding a new location — staff onboarding, rota access, HR records, labour reporting — is configured in minutes, not days. And as supplier chain automation is built in future phases, opening a new site will follow the same pattern: new suppliers onboarded like new staff, new kitchens operational faster, less admin drag on the people who should be focused on the food. This isn't bespoke work that breaks when you grow. It's infrastructure designed so that a new revenue-generating site can open with more action and less hassle than the last one. The retainer means Ben is with you at every step when it does.

Important context on scope

The £1,500–£3,000/month you're currently spending is for HR support alone. This build covers HR, staff communications, onboarding, payroll preparation and labour-cost reporting across all four sites — and establishes the automation infrastructure for everything that follows. The same architecture that onboards a new staff member in minutes can onboard a new supplier. The same logic that tracks a new starter's right-to-work can track a supplier's seasonal availability, order history and invoice status. A custom supplier CRM — built on the same stack, speaking to the same systems — becomes a natural Phase 2. You're not buying a fix for one problem. You're buying an approach that compounds.

The hidden cost nobody talks about
Every new starter costs far more than their first wage.

Hospitality turnover runs at 60–80% annually. In a business growing across four sites, that means you're processing new starters constantly — and every single one carries a true cost well beyond what the outsourced HR invoice shows. When that person leaves after two or three weeks, every penny of that onboarding cost is gone.
